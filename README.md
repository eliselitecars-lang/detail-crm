# Detail CRM

All-in-one CRM for auto detailing, ceramic coating, tint and PPF shops:
online booking, scheduling, customers & vehicles, quotes, invoices, Stripe
payments, memberships, inspections, photos, digital forms, team & time
tracking, two-way texting, automated reminders and reports — on the web and
iPhone, backed by one Supabase database.

Status: under active construction. See `docs/SPEC.md` for the full product
and architecture spec.
